Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My WOD Coach handles information when you use the app. The app provides personalized CrossFit-style workout planning based on your profile, records, and training logs.

Information we collect

  • Account information used for sign-in, such as an identifier provided through Apple, Google, or Supabase Auth.
  • Training information entered in the app, including training level, available workout time, weakness movements, personal records, workout notes, workout completion status, and workout history.
  • Technical information needed to operate the app, such as authentication session state and database records associated with your account.

How we use information

  • To provide app functionality, including sign-in, profile persistence, WOD planning, workout history, and account deletion.
  • To personalize WOD plans based on your settings, records, weakness movements, and completed workout logs.
  • To maintain reliability, security, and continuity of your app experience.

Data sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We use service providers such as Supabase for authentication and database storage, and OpenAI-powered generation infrastructure for personalized WOD planning when the app generates training content.
  • Data may be processed when required to operate the app, comply with law, or protect the app and its users.

Data retention and deletion

  • We retain account and training data while your account is active so the app can provide personalized plans and history.
  • You can request deletion through the account deletion feature in the app settings.
  • After account deletion, associated app records are removed or anonymized according to the app backend deletion flow.

Health and training notice

  • My WOD Coach provides fitness planning support and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
  • You are responsible for scaling workouts safely and consulting a qualified professional before starting or changing intense training.
