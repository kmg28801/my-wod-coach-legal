Support

My WOD Coach is a personalized CrossFit-style WOD planning app. If you need help with sign-in, WOD generation, saved records, workout history, or account deletion, contact support.

Contact

Email kmg08801@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Common checks

  • Confirm you are using the latest TestFlight or App Store build.
  • Check that your Apple or Google sign-in account is active.
  • Refresh the app after changing training settings or records.
  • Use the in-app account deletion flow if you want to delete your account and training data.
